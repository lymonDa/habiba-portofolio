I’m Habeeba. My journey started in childhood, photographing nature, sunrises and sunsets — and seeing how those small moments could inspire the people around me made me keep going.

Today I balance my engineering degree with visual storytelling, keeping the moments, people and details that others might walk past. Everything is shot on a phone — no studio, no shortcuts, just attention.
